Setup and reward

Each episode has 8 four-second chunks, two bitrates (2.85 and 4.3 Mbps), and a 60-second buffer. Playback stalls when buffered video runs out.

Return is the expected total reward per episode, averaged across 7 constant-bandwidth network traces: 3, 3.5, 4, 4.5, 5, 5.5, and 6 Mbps.

Reward per chunk = bitrate / 4.3 − stall penalty × stall seconds / 4

How RL learns

With parameters θ=(w,b), buffer state s=B/60 (B in seconds), and sigmoid σ:

πθ(high∣s) =σ(ws+b)

REINFORCE uses N=16 episodes of T=8 chunks to estimate the policy gradient:

g^(θ)= 1NT ∑i=1N ∑t=0T−1 Gt(i) ∇θlog πθ( at(i)∣ st(i)) g^(θ)= 1NT ∑i=1N ∑t=0T−1 Gt(i) ×∇θlog πθ( at(i)∣ st(i))
Gt(i)= ∑k=tT−1 rk(i)

at is the bitrate choice; rt is the chunk reward. Updates use Adam with a small entropy bonus.

Training runs anew in your browser. The heatmap shows evaluated policies, not saved training paths.

Failure modes

F1: Unstable optimization paths. A high-return ridge exists, but optimization misses it.

F2: Distorted return landscapes. Reward or trace changes shift the high-return region toward undesirable policies.
