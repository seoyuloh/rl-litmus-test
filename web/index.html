How reward works

Reward per chunk = bitrate / 4.3 − stall penalty × stall seconds / 4

Higher bitrate earns more reward; waiting for video subtracts reward. Bitrate is in Mbps, and each chunk is 4 seconds.

Return on the heatmap is the expected sum of rewards over 8 chunks, averaged across 7 constant-bandwidth network traces: 3, 3.5, 4, 4.5, 5, 5.5, and 6 Mbps.

Plot guide

Heatmap: the axes are policy weight w and bias b. Color shows expected return; the orange line traces the training updates.

Policy: the curves show each bitrate’s selection probability at the chosen step. Buffer occupancy of 100% means 60 seconds of video ready to play.

Controls: drag the timeline to inspect earlier steps. Each run stops at 10,000 steps. Train again starts fresh. Reset, a setting change, or a heatmap click resets training.
