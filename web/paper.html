Rediscover a rule, diagnose learning

Can reinforcement learning recover a familiar heuristic? For adaptive bitrate streaming, the rule is simple: favor higher quality as the playback buffer fills.

The paper uses compact policies to inspect the return landscape, optimization path, and correctness properties. It identifies two failure modes:

  • F1: Unstable optimization paths. A high-return ridge exists, but optimization misses it.
  • F2: Distorted return landscapes. Reward or trace changes shift the high-return region toward undesirable policies.
