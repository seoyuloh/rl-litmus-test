Can reinforcement learning recover a familiar heuristic? For adaptive bitrate streaming, the rule is simple: favor higher quality as the playback buffer fills.

The paper uses small, inspectable policies to connect the reward landscape, training path, and learned behavior. This separates two causes of failure:
